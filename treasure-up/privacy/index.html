Privacy Policy

Treasure Up — Eagle Ridge Tech LLC

Effective Date: October 28, 2026

Overview

Treasure Up is a lesson-preparation and teaching companion for Sunday School teachers, published by Eagle Ridge Tech LLC ("we," "us," or "our"). This policy explains what information is involved when you use Treasure Up and how it is handled. The short version: Treasure Up has no accounts, collects no personal information, and keeps everything you write on your own device.

No account, no personal information

Treasure Up does not require or offer an account, sign-in, or registration. We do not ask for, collect, or receive your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or any other personal information.

Your content stays on your device

Everything you create or change in Treasure Up is stored only on your device, including:

  • Lesson notes and experiences
  • Journal entries and reflections
  • Plan arrangements (section and card order, hidden items, added backups, swapped and follow-up questions)
  • Reading progress
  • Settings and preferences (such as default audience, class length, team-teaching weeks, appearance, text size, and reminder times)

This content is never transmitted to us or to any server we operate. It is included in your device's own backup (iCloud Backup on iOS, or Android Auto Backup on Android) according to your device's backup settings, which are governed by Apple or Google. Your content is not synced between devices: notes and plans on one phone do not appear on another. Deleting the app removes this content from your device; copies may remain in your device backups until those backups are replaced or deleted.

When you export slides (PowerPoint or PDF), the file is created on your device and shared only where you choose through your device's share sheet.

No analytics, advertising, or tracking

Treasure Up does not include analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking software. We do not display ads, we do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell, rent, or share data about you with anyone.

Purchases and subscriptions

Treasure Up Plus subscriptions are purchased through Apple's App Store (and, on Android, Google Play). Payment is processed entirely by Apple or Google; we never receive, store, or have access to your payment details. We use RevenueCat to confirm whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier generated on your device, along with purchase receipts from the store, so it can report your subscription status to the app. This identifier is not linked to your name or any other personal information.

  • Apple's privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

Scripture and links to other websites

Scripture text shown in Treasure Up comes from public-domain sources and is built into the app, so it works without a connection. Chapter, song, and media links open churchofjesuschrist.org in your web browser. That website is operated by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, not by us, and is subject to its own privacy notice: churchofjesuschrist.org/legal/privacy-notice.

Prep reminders

If you turn on prep reminders, they are scheduled as local notifications on your device. No push notification server is used, and no information about your reminders or teaching schedule leaves your device. You can turn reminders off in the app at any time, or disable notifications in your device settings.

Data deletion

Because we do not hold any of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. You can remove your content at any time by deleting it in the app or by deleting the app. To cancel a subscription, use your Apple ID (or Google Play) subscription settings.

Children

Treasure Up is intended for adult teachers preparing lessons. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time, for example if the app gains new features. The effective date at the top of this page will reflect when the most recent changes were made. If a change materially affects how your information is handled, we will also note it in the app. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
