Privacy Policy

IntuiPlate — Eagle Ridge Tech LLC

Effective Date: May 20, 2026

Overview

IntuiPlate is a personal food accountability and wellness tracking app. This policy explains what information is involved when you use IntuiPlate and how it is handled. An account is required to use the app so your data syncs securely across devices.

Information stored in your account

IntuiPlate stores your food entries, body metrics (weight, body fat, sleep), daily scores, and goal settings in a secure cloud database. This data is associated with your account and is used solely to provide the app's features to you. We do not use your personal health or food data for marketing, advertising, or any purpose other than delivering the app's functionality.

Account and authentication

IntuiPlate uses Sign in with Apple for authentication. We receive a unique identifier and, if you choose to share it, your name and email address from Apple. Your email address is used solely for account identification and support. We do not use it for marketing purposes. Authentication is handled by Supabase, a cloud database and authentication platform. Supabase's privacy policy is available at supabase.com/privacy.

Health and body data

IntuiPlate stores weight, body fat percentage, and sleep duration that you enter manually or that is synced from Apple Health (IntuiPlate Pro feature). This data is stored securely in your account and is used solely to display your trends and progress within the app. We do not share, sell, or transmit your health data to any third party except as described below.

Apple Health integration (Pro feature)

If you grant permission, IntuiPlate reads weight, body fat percentage, and sleep data from Apple Health on your device. This data is read locally and stored in your IntuiPlate account. We do not write data back to Apple Health. You can revoke this permission at any time in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access.

Camera and photo access

IntuiPlate may request access to your camera and photo library for the AI food scan feature. Photos taken or selected for food scanning are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to analyze the food and suggest scoring values. Photos are not stored by us after processing. Anthropic processes images according to their own privacy policy, available at anthropic.com/privacy. Images are transmitted securely via HTTPS.

AI food scan and weekly insights (Pro features)

When you use the AI food scan feature, your food photo is sent to Anthropic's Claude API. When weekly AI insights are generated, a summary of your food scoring data (aggregated numbers only — not food names or personal details) is sent to Anthropic's Claude API. This data is used solely to produce the requested output and is not stored by us after processing.

Subscription and payment information

IntuiPlate Pro subscriptions are managed through Apple's App Store. Payment processing is handled entirely by Apple — we never receive, store, or have access to your credit card number or payment details. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier to track whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Information we do not collect

IntuiPlate does not collect your phone number or precise location. We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not display advertising of any kind.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. The only external data transmission occurs during AI features (as described above), cloud storage via Supabase, and subscription verification through RevenueCat.

Data deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at the address below. We will process deletion requests within 30 days. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account data.

Children

IntuiPlate is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will reflect when the most recent changes were made. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
